PROCESS NOV 18, 2024

THE MAKING OF ZINE NO. 01

It started with a printer that kept jamming. Three months ago we decided we were going to make a zine — a real one, hand-numbered, limited to 100 copies, nothing digital. We had no idea what we were doing.

The first week was just arguing about size. Half of us wanted A5, half wanted A4 folded. We ended up with A4 folded because the copy shop near the rehearsal space only had one paper cutter and it was already scratched to hell.

"If it looks too clean it's not ours." — that was the rule we kept coming back to.

THE CONTENT

We gave everybody a week to submit whatever they wanted — lyrics, drawings, photographs, a short piece of writing. No brief, no theme. The result was chaotic in the best way: a half-finished tour diary next to a page of charcoal drawings next to three paragraphs about the smell of a venue at 3am.

We laid it all out in a borrowed copy of InDesign (free trial, expired twice). Every time we thought we were done, someone would find a typo or decide a photo needed to be bigger or smaller. The final layout took eleven hours on a single Saturday.

THE PRINTING

We printed at a copy shop two blocks from the venue where we played our first show. The guy behind the counter didn't ask questions. He printed 100 sets, we collated them by hand on the counter while he watched a football match on his phone.

Numbering took the longest. 100 copies, each one signed. By number 60 the pen was running out. We found another one in someone's jacket pocket. By number 88 we were laughing at nothing. By 100 we just sat there.

WHAT WE LEARNED

You don't need to know what you're doing. You need a printer, paper, and people who care enough to stay up late arguing about margins. The object that came out of it is imperfect in all the right ways. Pages that aren't quite straight. Ink that bled a little on the darker sections. Our fingerprints, literally, on some of the copies.

Zine No. 02 is already in the planning stage. We know a little more now. We're going to forget most of it on purpose.
